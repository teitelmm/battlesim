    How to play

    1. Pick a team and a unit from the palette at the bottom.
    2. Click the field to place one, or click-drag to paint a group.
    3. Build both armies, then press ▶ Start and enjoy the chaos.
    4. Reset puts everyone back so you can tweak and try again.

    On touch screens: tap or drag to place, two fingers to rotate and zoom.
